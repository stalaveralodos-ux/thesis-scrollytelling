The Constitutional Role of Municipalities in the Governance of Social Rights

Here's a paradox: cities are expected to deliver housing, education, and healthcare, yet the constitutional frameworks that define social rights were rarely written with municipalities in mind.

This is a comparative study of Turin, Barcelona, and Athens, three cities shaped by three different constitutional traditions, each improvising its own response to the same structural gap between what national constitutions promise and what local governments are actually equipped to deliver.
