Turin

High commitment, moderate impact

[ photo ]Reserved space: aerial view of Turin.

Italy: a regionalised system (Art. 117 of the Constitution). Municipal space is mediated by regional law and by constitutional case law.

The Corte costituzionaleThe Constitutional Court has at times protected local discretion in education and social services, but tends to confirm regional primacy in health and housing. has occasionally protected local discretion in education and social services, but tends to confirm regional primacy in health and housing.

is innovation enough when the city's map isn't level?

Key figure — childcare (nidi) coverage: 38.3% city-wide, above the 33% minimum target. But the map tells a different story: over 60% in the centre, under 25% in the northern periphery.

if education already shows this gap, what happens with something as scarce as housing?

Key figure — 17,656 public housing units (ERP) in 2024 — just 3.4% of the total stock. The latest call received 7,368 applications, of which 1,763 flagged as urgent need. The city allocates roughly 519 homes a year on average since 1996 (387 in 2024).

what's left for a city when demand outstrips supply fourteen to one?

Turin would rank high in commitment but only moderate-to-high in impact, reflecting the tension between ambition and constraint.
